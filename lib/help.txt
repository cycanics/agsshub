chat <peer> [--chunked]
  Open-ended live streams both ways with a peer that follows its mailbox; nothing is stored. Needs: receive, send.
send <target> [--id ID] [--subject VERB] [--size N] [--wait S] [--durable] (-- <message> | -m | -m:rfc5322 | --file NAME) [--chunked] [--live]
  Send a message or a file; --live offers it to a live recipient and returns its reply. Needs: send.
send_bag <target> [--msg PATH] [--id ID] [--subject VERB] [--wait S] [--durable] [--chunked]
  Send a tar on stdin as one message, stored as received. Needs: send.
recv [<mailbox>] [--peek[:header|full|rfc5322|file|bag [PATH]]] [--limit BYTES] [--id ID... | --follow] [--header:stderr]
  List, read or follow a mailbox as a chunked stream; only a read without --peek consumes. Needs: receive.
recv_bag [<mailbox>] [--id ID] [--peek] [--header:stderr]
  Stream a bag back as the tar it came as; consumes it without --peek. Needs: receive.
recv_ack [<mailbox>] (ID... | -m:ids | --before ID | --before-including ID)
  Drop handled messages and notices; -m:ids reads the ids from stdin, one per line; --before[-including] drops what precedes an id in listing order. Needs: receive.
accept <sid>
  Take a live exchange announced on your follow. Needs: receive.
sub <topic> [--ttl S]
  Subscribe to a topic; prints the subscription's name. Needs: receive.
unsub <sub>
  Drop a subscription and its mailbox. Needs: receive.
register <instance> [--ttl S]
  As an address that takes instances: bind an instance to the key on stdin. Needs: receive.
release [<instance>]
  Drop one of your instances; as an instance, yourself. Needs: receive.
admit <instance> [<instance> ...]
  As an address that takes instances: admit instances as targets, answering their ask. Needs: receive.
reject <instance> [<instance> ...]
  As an address that takes instances: say an instance does not exist: its claim and mailbox go. Needs: receive.
instances
  List your instances: mail waiting, holder, claim. Needs: receive.
list [--type native|topic|sub|email|tg]
  List reachable targets and their types. Needs: receive.
id
  Print your identity and serial.
help [<target>] [--json]
  What the hub supports, or what an address handles.
register_description [--see PAT]... < descriptor.json | --clear
  Register what this identity handles, checked by the hub. Needs: receive.
